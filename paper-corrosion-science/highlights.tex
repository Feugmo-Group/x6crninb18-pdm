Identifiability of a point defect model for oxide growth on AISI~347
\end{center}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=4pt]
\item Point defect model identified for duplex oxide on AISI~347 in reactor water
\item Profile likelihood leaves one of five fitted parameters undetermined
\item 95\% of the fit statistic rests on three points, and 80\% on a single one
\item Power law and mechanistic fits agree on data but differ 3.5--6.3x at ten years
\item An exposure beyond 3000 h would discriminate the two extrapolations
\end{itemize}
